FAQs

Oral Surgery Wisdom Teeth: Frequently Asked Questions

When should wisdom teeth be removed?

Removal is often recommended when wisdom teeth are impacted, infected, decayed, crowding other teeth or causing pain. An exam and X-ray help us determine the right timing for you.

Is wisdom tooth removal painful?

We use anesthesia options to keep you comfortable during the procedure. Mild soreness afterward is normal and can usually be managed with the aftercare instructions we provide.

What is the difference between simple and surgical extraction?

A simple extraction removes a tooth that has fully erupted. A surgical extraction is used for impacted or partially erupted teeth and may involve a small incision in the gum.

How long does recovery take?

Many patients feel better within a few days, though full healing can take longer depending on the case. We'll give you personalized aftercare guidance, including diet and cleaning tips.

Do you offer emergency wisdom tooth removal in Bensalem?

Yes. If you have severe pain, swelling or signs of infection, call 267-539-9219 as soon as possible so we can advise you on the next steps.
